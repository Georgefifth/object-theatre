# Design decisions

Subject: everyday tabletop objects staging a short, unruly play. Audience: first-time visitors who want a two-minute creative interaction.

Tokens: stage white #ffffff, violet ink #342254, protest pink #bc2450, ticket yellow #f5d46c, quiet violet #62536c, light paper tint #f1eaf7. Trebuchet MS plus Chinese system sans fallbacks; bold compact title, readable dialogue, quiet supporting text. No downloaded font dependency.

Layout: left-aligned opening invitation and object speech; direct sample action; optional casting; four-panel stage; actor interruption and two choices.

```
[ Theatre                     Language ]
[ Objects have a say.     talking cup  ]
[ Play the sample                     ]
[ Change the cast (expand)             ]
             after opening
[ Story title              Take away  ]
[ panel 1             | panel 2        ]
[ panel 3             | panel 4        ]
[ actor objects       | two choices   ]
```

Brief review: the prior giant title and cream page could resemble a generic landing page. Retain the established violet object illustrations, shift the base to stage white, remove the single-word headline colour treatment, and put the creative risk into the actor interruption. Casting is supporting structure, not a row of obligatory cards. Mobile reading becomes sequential, trading a longer scroll for legible dialogue. No new autonomous animations; reduced motion also applies to scrolling.

Success is finishing and understanding a short play. Cross-language quality and repeat engagement still require user feedback.
